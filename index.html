Stanford PwdHash

Generates theft-resistant, site-specific passwords — computed entirely in your browser, nothing is ever sent anywhere.

Install the browser extension to generate these passwords invisibly as you browse: Firefox · Chrome. Press F2 or prefix passwords with @@ to activate.

Stanford project · USENIX 2005 paper · source
